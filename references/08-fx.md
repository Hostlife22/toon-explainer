# 08 · FX v4: materials, modifiers, fights, stand-ins, booms, rivalry, light and grades

These v4 effects come from the second reference pass, which covered empire, horror, myth and war explainers. Each one
is deterministic and seeded by the layer `id`, so give every effect layer an `id`. An `id` does not make `fx` layers blur under a shot's rack `focus`: they stay sharp unless they set `focusable: true` ([03](03-spec-reference.md)). Boiling parts re-seed on twos.

Showcase: [examples/v4_fx/spec.json](../examples/v4_fx/spec.json), with one shot per feature.
Code: `scripts/engine/lib/fx4.js` (fx kinds, `fg: mob`, grades, `T.drawBrawl`) and `scripts/engine/lib/mods.js`
(layer modifiers, ghost tail, plus the fx kinds that repaint another layer: `shock`, `disintegrate` and `memory`).

## Layer modifiers (any layer type)

A modifier turns on when its field is set on the layer. The layer is painted offscreen and then post-processed, and
modifiers stack in this order: `silhouette` 10, `lineart` 20, `material` 30, `freeze` 32, `split` 60, `sticker` 80,
`glow` 90, `blur` 95. Every modifier also works inside `swap`, so `{"at": 1.0, "material": "stone"}` makes a statue in
one frame.

| Field | Values (defaults) | Look and motion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `material: "ghost"` | `{kind, alpha 0.55, tint #b9c2c8, rim #eef3f6, bob 5, wisp true}` | Desaturated, tinted and see-through, with a pale rim and a ±5 px bob at 0.5 Hz. A ghost bean loses its legs and gets an S-shaped wisp tail that sways at 0.8 Hz (set `wisp: false` or `legs: true` to keep the legs). Pair it with `enter: "fade"` and `glow`. |
| `material: "stone"` | `{kind, color #c9bea8, cracks 3, crack #8f846f, plinth false, plinthColor}` | Grey-beige statue with seeded cracks. `plinth` adds a stone block under the feet. Set `rock: 0` and `blink: false` on the bean. |
| `material: "gold"` | `{kind, ramp [shadow, mid, highlight], glints 4}` | Gold luminance ramp with four twinkling glints. |
| `material: "engrave"` | `{kind, ramp, alpha 0.9}` | Sepia engraving: ink lines turn light, fills turn dark brown, faint grain, and the paper shows through. For a gold or full-colour accent, leave that one layer without the material (a crown in an engraved tableau). |
| `material: "ice"` | `{kind, tint #bfe8ea}` | Frosted look without the block. |
| *(all materials)* | `at`, `dur 0.5` | Crossfade from the plain layer starting at `at` (shot time). |
| `freeze` | `{at 0, dur 0.2, pad 0.08, tint, color}` | Frosted figure inside a faceted ice block: translucent front face, lighter top and darker side faces in a shallow three-quarter view, ink edges, facet lines and highlight streaks. Fades in over 6 frames with a 2 % pop, then holds. Stop the motion at `at`, for example `jitter: {to: at}`. |
| `sticker` | `true` or `{width 6, color #fff, shadow 0.25, hover false}` | Die-cut white border with a soft drop shadow. `hover` adds a ±3 px bob every 0.8 s and a 1 px boil. Pair it with `enter: "pop-tilt"`. |
| `split` | `{at, gap 0.18, angle 90, jag 0.06, rot 6, label: {text, color #c8202a}}` | A crack line appears 1 frame before `at`. At `at` the layer squashes for 1 frame, then the jagged halves spring apart over 3 frames (outBack) and rotate ±`rot`°, with a ribbon label in the gap. `angle` 90 is a vertical cut. |

When to use them:

- "He died / his ancestors watched / he returned to haunt them": use `ghost`.
- "He became a legend / a statue was raised": swap to `stone` with a plinth.
- "In the age of myth": put an `engrave` tableau on `bg: parchment`.
- "Rich beyond measure": use `gold`.
- "Froze to death / frozen out / put on ice": use `freeze` or `ice`.
- Characters on a map or a busy background: use `sticker`.
- "The alliance broke / heartbroken / divided": use `split` on a heart prop, a flag or a character.

## FX kinds (`"type": "fx"`)

Full-frame kinds use frame coordinates. Give them `"hud": true` to keep them off the camera. Kinds that take a
`target` or `follow` read that layer from the same shot by `id`.

| `kind` | Fields (defaults) | Look and timing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `brawl` | `size 480, path [[x,y],...], travel, dur, end pop/puffs, limbs [fist, boot, sword, spear], skins [hex or cast names], stars 5, haze false, dust true` | White fight cloud with fists, boots and weapons poking out, helmet heads peeking over the edge, and an angry face. Everything re-seeds on twos. `path` moves the cloud linearly over `travel` (default `dur`). After `dur` the cloud pops out over 6 frames or breaks into 6 puffs. Hide the fighters with `out` at the fx `at`, then show the outcome (the winner standing, the loser posed `lie` with `dead` eyes). |
| `blood-wipe` | `from bottom/top/both, color #8a0a0a, count 9, drips 5, dur 2, cover false` | Flame-like red tongues rise in 3 frames and boil on twos. The scene stays visible between them, and thin drips grow 6 px per frame from the opposite edge. `cover` fills the frame over the last 0.2 s, so you can cut on it. |
| `blood-streaks` | `count 7, color #8a0a14, inner #c02030` | Diagonal tapered brush strokes. Each one sweeps in over 2 frames, staggered by 1 frame. Hold for about 10 frames, then cut to black. |
| `blood-splash` | `from left/right, count 6, spread 220, size 1` | Teardrops (with trailing droplets) fly in on decelerating arcs over 10 frames, then land as spiky outlined splats ringed by droplets; each grows a drip that runs 60 px down over 1.5 s. The first splat is 1.6× larger. |
| `pool` | `size 260, dur 6, color #5a0a10, flat 0.32` | Flat lobed pool with an ink outline and one shade tone that spreads (outCubic) under a body. Put it before the bean in the layer list; use `size` 400+ under a 500 px bean so it reads. |
| `spill` | `size 300, dur 2, color #7a1420, tendrils 0, flood false, flat 1` | Stain that spreads as one ink-outlined silhouette (core and tendrils share the outline) with a shade tone and a gloss highlight; tendrils grow 1.4× faster. `flood` keeps growing until the frame is covered by `dur` + 1.5 s. Ink preset: `#2b2050`. Put the title label after the spill so it stays readable over the flood. |
| `shadow-hands` | `count 5, dur 1.2, alpha 0.55, weapons true, darken 0.4` | Reaching arm shadows with clawed four-finger hands and a thumb (with `weapons`, every other hand grips a spear) slide in from both edges (outCubic, staggered 0.1 s) while the frame darkens, then sway 2 px at 2 Hz and flex the fingers on twos. All arms composite as one shadow, so overlaps do not darken. |
| `shock` | `target, dur 0.8, color #4fd2ff, xray true, spikes true, count 6` | The target cycles normal / white / black x-ray (skull, spine and ribs) / half alpha on a seeded 2–3 frame rhythm. Zigzag bolts and a starburst re-seed every 2 frames, and the hair stands up. Hide the target for the window: `swap: [{at, alpha: 0}, {at: at + dur, alpha: 1, expr: "dead"}]`. |
| `disintegrate` | `target, pieces 20, ash true, stinger false` | Optional 2-frame blue negative stinger. Shards drift out over 4 frames, then turn black and fall into an ash pile with a smoke stem over 12 frames. Hide the target from the fx `at`. |
| `boom` | `size 420, style puff/mushroom, debris 8, text` | Puff: frame 0 spark; frames 1–15 a white-hot core grows; frames 16–30 it turns yellow, then orange, and debris flies; frames 31–42 brown smoke and embers fade. Pop a second boom 9 frames later elsewhere. Mushroom: a stem and cap grow over 15 frames, hold, and fade by frame 72. `text` turns it into a full-frame interruption card (yellow explosion cloud with a dark rim and a white disc of heavy text; use `\n` for line breaks). |
| `onomatopoeia` | `text BOOOM, size 360, score, dur 1.5` | Grey smoke cloud with a flickering red-orange-yellow star and bouncing graffiti letters. Scales 0.8 → 1.1 over 12 frames and fades over the last 8. `score` rises green with an overshoot at frame 4. |
| `word-shatter` | `text, color #d8242a, size 140, angle -18, vx 240, burstAt 0.9` | Extruded word pops in (0.4 → 1.1 → 1.0 by frame 5) and drifts at `vx` px/s, turning 0.4° per frame. At `burstAt` every glyph flies off with spin and gravity behind a yellow puff cluster. |
| `crackle` | `from [x,y] or layer id, to [x,y] or layer id, color #ffe14a, width 4, glow 12` | Jagged glowing line, re-seeded every frame, with a spark at the midpoint. With two bean layer ids it runs eye to eye as a bold stare beam (width 14, glow 26) that follows the beans; place it after them. |
| `beam-clash` | `from, to, color #7fe6ff, bg #2a2650, flash true` | Full-frame rivalry backdrop: speed streaks, two jagged beams and an orb that drifts ±80 px (1.3 s period) and toggles orb / ring / sparkle every 2–3 frames. 5-frame white flash at the start. Put it right after the bg. |
| `duel` | `left 0.22, right 0.78, h 0.62, rim #6b4a8a, eyes [idA, idB] / [[x,y],[x,y]] / false, width 14, spark` | Two black masses bristling with blades behind the rivals, an inner rim light, and a bold stare beam between the eyes (bean ids anchor it to the eyes: head centre ~(0, -450) rig units, eyes at y -458). Put it before the beans; since the beans then cover the beam ends, the showcase sets `eyes: false` and adds a `crackle` with `from`/`to` ids after the beans. |
| `lightpool` | `follow <id>, part hand/head/center, r 420, color #ffd04a, dark 0.85, open {at, dur 0.66}` | Darkness with a warm hole that follows a character. `open` grows the hole from 60 px (glow-open). The radius flickers ±5 % on twos. |
| `shafts` | `items [{x, w, angle}], bars 4, color #fff4d0, alpha 0.28` | Soft window light with faint bar shadows that fade out by mid-frame, so they never read as cage bars over a character. `x` is the floor end. Draw it over the characters. |
| `godbeam` | `from tl/tr/t, target [x,y], width 220, color #fff6b0, alpha 0.6` | One feathered beam that grows over 10 frames, with a glow at the target. |
| `spotcone` | `x 0.5, top 0, floor 0.86, w 0.32, alpha 0.18, dark 0.85` | Stage cone and floor ellipse. Everything outside is darkened. |
| `memory` | `who, pose, expr, look, view, flip, x 0.75, y 0.9, size 520, alpha 0.85, tint #8fa6d8, backdrop fire/rays, bubble true, tail [x, y], dur 0.66` | A tinted cast bean inside an outlined thought bubble (pops in over `dur`, three shrinking circles trail toward `tail`, default the frame centre), with the optional fire or rays backdrop clipped inside the bubble behind it. `bubble: false` gives the old free-floating ghost (alpha 0.45). `{shot, t, rect}` shows a frozen frame of another shot in an ink-framed panel instead. `{who, face: true, scale 1.6, expr "evil", dim 0.45}` darkens the scene and adds a grey giant face (a dark thought). |

When to use them:

- "They fought / civil war / a scuffle": use `brawl`.
- "Massacre / killed / sacrificed": use `blood-wipe`, `blood-streaks`, `blood-splash` or `pool`. Always imply it, never show it.
- "The people revolted": use `fg: mob`.
- "Doom closed in": use `shadow-hands`.
- "Zapped / struck down": use `shock`.
- "His zeal crumbled": use `disintegrate`.
- "It blew up": use `boom`.
- "We don't have time" interruption gag: use `boom` with `text`.
- An achievement, or a score-like line: use `onomatopoeia`.
- "The orders never arrived / the deal collapsed": use `word-shatter`.
- "Rivals / a battle of wills": use `duel` or `beam-clash`.
- "Into the darkness": use `lightpool`.
- "Imprisoned": use `shafts`.
- "A miracle": use `godbeam`.
- "Alone with it": use `spotcone`.
- "Corruption spread / the ink ruined it": use `spill`.
- "He remembered / a dark thought": use `memory`.

## Foreground: `"type": "fg", "kind": "mob"`

`items [pitchfork, torch, spear, scimitar, banner, arm], count 8, color #141010, h [200, 320], rise 0.27, sway 8`.
A two-row crowd of head-and-shoulders silhouettes (ears, caps and brimmed hats; the back row smaller and higher) rises
from below the frame, each with one arm raised and bent at the elbow, holding its tool. Each person takes 8 frames
(outCubic), and they start 2 frames apart. They then sway ±`sway` px on twos. Torch flames are the only coloured part.
The heads sit in the lower quarter of the frame, so keep the main character above y 0.65.

## Grade presets (`shot.grade`)

These presets work with the C10 grade entry fields `{preset, at, dur, strength, out, outDur, target}`. You can also
pass an array of entries to cycle grades.

| Preset | Use |
| --- | --- |
| `rage` | Anger mid-shot. Ramp it in over 0.5 s with `{preset: "rage", at, dur: 0.5}`. |
| `inferno` | The dark twin of a previous shot. |
| `golden-hour` | Departure, farewell, aftermath glory. |
| `moonlit` | Night secrets, grief. |
| `night-violet` | Night charge, dread. |
| `gaslight` | A writer at night, candle rooms. |
| `pink` | Romance, alliance, "aww". A strong rose wash with a magenta vignette. |
| `purple-neon` | A gamer's screen-lit room. |
| `crt-cyan` | Footage on a monitor, CCTV playback. Desaturated cyan with scanlines (pixel op `scanlines`, every other 2-row band at 1080p) and a dark vignette. |
| `dusk-blue` | Dignified tragedy, with no gags. |
| `red-flash` | A 4–6 frame impact (`at`, `out` = `at` + 0.17) or a 1 s realisation. |
| `sunset-epic` | Founding sagas, villains arriving. |

For a trauma cycle, chain `moonlit`, `gaslight` and `night-violet` with `out`/`at` about 28 frames apart (see shot
`grade_trauma` in the example).

## Exports for other modules

- `T.drawBrawl(ctx, x, y, size, lt, seed, {limbs, skins, stars, dust, line})` draws the fight cloud for map brawls.
- `T.layers.fx` passes the shot `env` to every `T.FX[kind](ctx, L, lt, env)`.

## Cost

At 1080p:

- `material`, `sticker`, `split` and `freeze` take one or two offscreen passes plus a 1/8-size alpha scan, about 5–15 ms each.
- `engrave`, `gold`, `rage` and `inferno` run a pixel pass, about 20–60 ms.
- `shock`, `disintegrate` and `memory` repaint one layer offscreen.

Use them on a few layers per shot.
