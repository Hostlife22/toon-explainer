# The Race to the Moon (landscape, 16:9)

Central question: two rival programs race for the Moon; does the one with the biggest rocket get there first?
Arc: escalation ladder with a twist. Rivals on one planet (hook) -> Red builds bigger and bigger, Blue builds small and checks twice -> years pass -> Red launches first and blows up on the pad (turn) -> Blue counts down and lifts off -> the engine dies halfway (near-failure) -> fixed with one punch -> touchdown -> payoff: from the Moon the two rivals are one small planet.
Recurring devices: personified rockets with faces; the opening globe returns as the final shot in a changed state; a quest chip FIRST ON THE MOON? that flips to a tick on touchdown.

**Shot 1**
- Voiceover: "Two rival space programs. One Moon. Only one can get there first."
- On screen: a whole globe in a starry sky; a red land and a blue land pop in with angry faces and flags; a double arrow RACE between them; title THE RACE TO THE MOON.

**Shot 2**
- Voiceover: "Red Program versus Blue Program."
- On screen: jagged VS card, the smug Red chief against the angry Blue chief.

**Shot 3**
- Voiceover: "Red built big. Then bigger. Blue built small, and checked everything twice."
- On screen: two rockets with faces on a field; the red one flexes and a poof turns it into a giant (BIGGER!); the blue chief checks her scroll and gives a thumbs-up (CHECKED TWICE).

**Shot 4**
- Voiceover: "Years went by."
- On screen: an accelerating day-night time-lapse (the sun wears shades) over the two rockets; engineers bustle; a calendar rolls from YEAR 1 to YEAR 8.

**Shot 5**
- Voiceover: "Red launched first. Gravity had other plans."
- On screen: the giant red rocket lifts on its flame, wobbles, snaps to shock and blows up in a mushroom cloud (uncrewed); the Red chief facepalms; kill feed GRAVITY -> RED ROCKET.

**Shot 6**
- Voiceover: "Blue's turn. Three, two, one..."
- On screen: mission control; the big screen shows the blue rocket on its pad live; engineers from behind; a giant T-3 ... T-0 countdown.

**Shot 7**
- Voiceover: "Liftoff!"
- On screen: the small blue rocket screams up out of frame on its flame; smoke floods the pad; WHOOSH; fans cheer in the foreground.

**Shot 8**
- Voiceover: "Halfway there, the engine died. So they fixed it the classic way."
- On screen: deep space, Earth below; the flame dies, a red flash, ENGINE FAILURE warning, the thrust meter drops; a giant boxing glove on a piston punches the rocket; the flame roars back, FIXED!, speed lines.

**Shot 9**
- Voiceover: "Touchdown. Blue was first on the Moon."
- On screen: grey Moon plain with Earth in the sky; the rocket settles in dust; the astronaut pops out, cheers, and a blue flag drops into the ground; TOUCHDOWN; the quest chip turns green.

**Shot 10**
- Voiceover: "And from up there, the two rivals looked like one small planet."
- On screen: the opening globe again; both lands turn the same green, their angry faces become heart eyes; ONE PLANET, SEEN FROM THE MOON.
