# Rise and Fall of a Startup (landscape, 16:9)

Central question: how does a company with a billion-dollar valuation end up back in the garage?
Arc: rise and fall in ten beats. Garage idea -> pitch -> investors bid -> the hockey-stick chart -> mass hiring -> press hype -> lavish perks (the turn hides inside the joke: every pick drains the cash) -> the money runs out -> funding never arrives -> payoff: the same garage, now with cobwebs, and the same idea, version 2.0.
Recurring devices: the cash counter in the top-left corner (`spec.overlays`) that climbs in shot 3 and drains to zero in shot 8; the garage set of shot 1 reused in an abandoned state for the ending.

**Shot 1** 🎬
- Voiceover: "Day one. A garage, a founder and one big idea."
- On screen: night garage, a monitor on a crate reads NAPR v0.1; the founder snaps from worried to star eyes and the idea bulb pops; label THE FOUNDER, tag DAY 1.

**Shot 2**
- Voiceover: "He pitched it on stage: an app that rates your naps."
- On screen: whip to a stage with spotlights and curtains; the founder presents, a shout bubble AI FOR NAPS!, the easel chart climbs; the audience is a silhouette row.

**Shot 3** 🎬
- Voiceover: "Investors fought to throw money at it."
- On screen: auction house LOT 1 NAPR; paddles 1M, 5M, 20M pop up; investors with dollar eyes; score popups; the cash counter appears and climbs to $20M.

**Shot 4** 🎬
- Voiceover: "Users went up. And up. A perfect hockey stick."
- On screen: zoom-punch to a line chart on grid paper; the line bends up, a moneybag lands on June, callout HOCKEY STICK!; punch on "stick".

**Shot 5**
- Voiceover: "So he hired. Everyone."
- On screen: the office fills with a marching crowd; a system log reads DEVELOPER JOINED, DESIGNER JOINED, CHIEF VIBES OFFICER JOINED; a CEO marker over the founder.

**Shot 6**
- Voiceover: "The papers called him a genius."
- On screen: swirl to two newspapers, 0.5 s apart: NAP APP WORTH $1 BILLION and GENIUS!; confetti.

**Shot 7** 🎬
- Voiceover: "He spent it on the important things."
- On screen: a game shop carousel snaps through GOLD THRONE, ARCADE, NAP PODS, MONEY POOL; the cash counter drops on every pick.

**Shot 8** 🎬
- Voiceover: "Then the money ran out. Fast."
- On screen: black beat, then the office at night in a cold grade; a vertical CASH meter drains, 30 DAYS LEFT spins down to 0, three error popups stack (PAYROLL, SERVERS, COFFEE); the founder screams.

**Shot 9**
- Voiceover: "The next round of funding is still loading."
- On screen: a monitor with a spinner "Series B.."; the funding bar crawls to 99 % and stops; the kill feed: MARKET bombs VALUATION, INVESTORS surrender NAPR, LANDLORD takes the OFFICE; the founder slumps.

**Shot 10** 🎬
- Voiceover: "Day one. Again."
- On screen: squeeze cut back to the garage of shot 1, now under cobwebs; label DAY 1 / AGAIN; the founder gets the same idea for NAPR 2.0; game-over card $1B BURNED, CONTINUE? 3.
