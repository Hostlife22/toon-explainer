# Why Ice Floats (vertical short, 9:16)

Central question: why does frozen water float when almost every other frozen thing sinks?
Arc: a broken rule (hook) -> the mechanism acted out by molecules -> the consequence for a lake -> "what if it sank" -> payoff back on the drink.

**Shot 1** 🎬
- Voiceover: "Almost everything shrinks when it freezes."
- On screen: a candle bean melts and refreezes smaller; a tiny scientist squints at it. Visible change inside the first second.

**Shot 2** 🎬
- Voiceover: "Water does the opposite."
- On screen: an ice cube's shadow grows, the cube drops into a tall glass and bobs back up to the top.

**Shot 3**
- Voiceover: "That's why ice floats, and why lakes don't freeze solid."
- On screen: a whip down from the mug to under a frozen lake: a lid of ice blocks on top, a swimmer bobbing below waves.

**Shot 4** 🎬
- Voiceover: "Here's the trick. Liquid water is a crowd: packed tight, pushing, always moving."
- On screen: swirl into a tight crowd of six blue molecule blobs jostling and shoving; pinned header LIQUID.

**Shot 5** 🎬
- Voiceover: "Cool it down, and the molecules slow enough to hold hands."
- On screen: the same six blobs (no re-entrance) calm down as the grade cools; arms sprout one after another.

**Shot 6** 🎬
- Voiceover: "But they only hold hands at fixed angles, so they build rings with an empty middle."
- On screen: the same six blobs snap into a ring one by one; EMPTY SPACE sits in the hole.

**Shot 7** 🎬
- Voiceover: "Same molecules, more room. Ice takes about nine percent more space."
- On screen: stacked comparison, the liquid crowd on top, the larger ice ring below; counter +9% between them.

**Shot 8** 🎬
- Voiceover: "Bigger but not heavier, so it's lighter for its size, and it rises."
- On screen: a block of ice rises through water past the label LESS DENSE.

**Shot 9**
- Voiceover: "In winter that ice becomes a lid on the lake."
- On screen: whip up to the lake surface freezing over.

**Shot 10** 🎬
- Voiceover: "Under the lid, the water stays around four degrees, and life gets through the winter."
- On screen: whip down under the ice; the swimmer and a blob fish wave; counter 4 degrees C.

**Shot 11** 🎬
- Voiceover: "If ice sank, lakes would freeze from the bottom up. Solid."
- On screen: negative flash; the same underwater view turns to ice, the swimmer frozen in a block.

**Shot 12**
- Voiceover: "So the next time your drink clinks, that's a tiny lid, saving a lake."
- On screen: swirl back to the mug from shot 2 as a print on a table; the frame drains to grey on the last words; hard stop.
