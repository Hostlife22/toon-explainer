# Who Left the Banana Peel? (landscape, 16:9)

Central question: the most important man in town slips on a banana peel at his own grand opening. Who left it there?
Arc: case loop with a surprise culprit. The victim struts in (the peel is already visible) -> the slip -> a detective gets the case -> clue 1: the dusty ledger of banana buyers -> clue 2: the evidence board ties three suspects to the peel -> the lineup -> the picker clears all three and lands on "???" -> the turn: the victim himself, interrogated, lies and his nose grows -> the evidence adds up on a comic page: the mayor dropped his own peel -> payoff: nobody picked the peel up, and the detective slips on it too.
Recurring devices: the banana peel (seen first, before anyone slips; the same peel ends the film); a SLIPS counter that stays on screen and goes 0 -> 1 -> 2; the town square reused at the start and the end.
Showcase: `banana-peel`, `ribbon`, `nameplate`, walking, `crowd`, jump + `upended` + hat `fly` + `pivot-fall`, `onomatopoeia`, `dust`, `whoosh` marks, giant `hand` with a `bracelet` label, hat `drop` with `bonk`, `cobweb` with a spider, `magnifier`, ledger `document`, `gaslight` grade, `corkboard` with red `strings`, `question` marks, `crowd` `lineup`, `spotlight`, `sweat`, `carousel` with `snap`, `spotcone` interrogation lamp, liar `nose`, `sweat-spray`, `jitter`, speech `bubble`, comic `grid` panel, `stamp`, `tear-stream`, `wail`, `tumble` exit with `detach`; transitions `cloud-wipe` with a word, `glow-open`, `slash`, `barwipe`, `peephole`, `flash-cut`, `iris`.

**Shot 1**
- Voiceover: "Grand opening day. Mayor Pompworth, the most important man in town."
- On screen: the town square under a GRAND OPENING ribbon; the mayor struts in; nameplate VERY IMPORTANT / MAYOR POMPWORTH; a banana peel already lies in his path; the crowd cheers.

**Shot 2** 🎬
- Voiceover: "Then: the peel."
- On screen: his foot hits the peel; he flies up flailing, his top hat spins away; WHOOPS!; he lands flat in a puff of dust; the counter goes to 1 SLIP.

**Shot 3** 🎬
- Voiceover: "City Hall wanted a culprit, so they called Inspector Crumb."
- On screen: an office; a giant cuffed hand labelled CITY HALL shoves a case file into the detective's hands; a deerstalker drops onto his head; nameplate DETECTIVE / INSPECTOR CRUMB.

**Shot 4**
- Voiceover: "First, the dusty archive: who bought bananas today?"
- On screen: a dim archive with cobwebs and a dangling spider; the detective sweeps a magnifier over the ledger BANANA SALES: TODAY (the chef, the gardener, the clown, and a smudged fourth line).

**Shot 5**
- Voiceover: "Three buyers. Three suspects. All connected to the peel."
- On screen: evidence board WHO DID IT?; red strings snap from THE PEEL to CHEF, GARDENER and CLOWN; the detective strokes his chin, a "?" over his head.

**Shot 6**
- Voiceover: "The usual suspects. Every one of them sweating."
- On screen: a diagonal lineup of the chef, the gardener and the clown under a hard spotlight; ALL THREE / SUSPECTS.

**Shot 7** 🎬
- Voiceover: "Chef? Alibi. Gardener? Alibi. Clown? Alibi. So who...?"
- On screen: a game-style SELECT SUSPECT picker snaps CHEF, GARDENER, CLOWN, then lands on a mystery card ???.

**Shot 8** 🎬
- Voiceover: "Mayor, do you eat bananas? Never! I hate bananas!"
- On screen: interrogation room; a lamp cone on the bandaged mayor; a giant finger points; I NEVER EAT BANANAS!; his nose grows; sweat sprays; LIAR!

**Shot 9** 🎬
- Voiceover: "The ledger. The peel. The nose. The mayor dropped it himself."
- On screen: a comic page of four frozen panels (THE LEDGER, THE PEEL, THE SLIP, THE NOSE); a stamp slams across it: GUILTY: THE MAYOR.

**Shot 10** 🎬
- Voiceover: "Case closed. Though somebody should really pick up that peel."
- On screen: the town square again; the mayor wails on the ground; CASE CLOSED; the smug detective strolls off, steps on the same peel and tumbles away, his hat left behind; NOT AGAIN!; the counter goes to 2 SLIPS.
