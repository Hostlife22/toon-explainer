# How a Vaccine Trains Your Body (landscape, 16:9)

Central question: how do you train an army for an enemy it has never met?
Arc: mechanism with a twist. An army with no idea what the enemy looks like (hook) -> scientists draw a harmless copy -> the vaccine is a wanted poster delivered to the troops -> the troops study it, build matching weapons and practise on a cardboard copy -> memory cells file the face -> months later the real virus arrives, sure of itself (turn) -> the guards recognise it -> the fight is over in a second -> payoff: the host slept through the whole war.
Recurring devices: white-cell guards in tin helmets and a spiky green virus played by the same cast in every shot; the WANTED poster returns as a memory and on the bedroom wall; a quest chip READY FOR THE REAL ONE? that flips on the knockout.

**Shot 1**
- Voiceover: "Your body has an army. It has never seen the enemy it must fight."
- On screen: inside the body, red blood cells drift past three helmeted white-cell guards; question marks pop over their heads; YOUR IMMUNE SYSTEM.

**Shot 2**
- Voiceover: "So scientists draw them a picture: the virus, made harmless."
- On screen: a blueprint; a spiky virus is sketched and labelled LIVE VIRUS; it is crossed out, the label is wiped and rewritten HARMLESS COPY; the board clears and a WANTED poster is sketched.

**Shot 3**
- Voiceover: "The vaccine is that picture: a wanted poster, delivered to the troops."
- On screen: the barracks corkboard; a WANTED poster of the virus pops up; stamp SAFE COPY; guards watch from behind.

**Shot 4**
- Voiceover: "They study its face, build a weapon that fits it, and practise."
- On screen: chalkboard lines type in (STUDY THE FACE, BUILD THE WEAPON, PRACTISE); the sergeant's picture bubble shows the face, an arrow, a shield, an arrow, a sword.

**Shot 5**
- Voiceover: "Practice, on a cardboard copy."
- On screen: a white wireframe training void; a cardboard cut-out of the virus; a spring glove pummels it three times; it topples over; +100 XP.

**Shot 6**
- Voiceover: "Then memory cells file the face away. For years."
- On screen: an old memory cell beside a file card FILE: SPIKY; ticks land on each line; stamp SAVED.

**Shot 7**
- Voiceover: "Six months later, the real virus shows up."
- On screen: a black beat SIX MONTHS LATER; a CHALLENGER APPROACHING card with the real virus, glowing eyes.

**Shot 8**
- Voiceover: "But the guards have seen that face before."
- On screen: the virus sneaks in; focus racks to a guard in close-up; the poster flashes as a memory; letterbox, a jump cut-in and a dutch tilt on the guard's rage; a stare beam; I KNOW YOU.

**Shot 9**
- Voiceover: "This time it is over in a second."
- On screen: a brawl cloud of helmets, fists and spikes; it bursts; K.O.; the quest chip turns green.

**Shot 10**
- Voiceover: "And you? You slept through the whole war."
- On screen: a bedroom at night; the host drools and snores; a system log reports VIRUS DETECTED, VIRUS REMOVED, HOST: STILL ASLEEP; the poster on the wall is stamped CAUGHT.
