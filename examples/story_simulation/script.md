# What If We Live in a Simulation? (landscape, 16:9)

Central question: if the world were a simulation, how would you notice, and who would be running it?
Arc: a thought experiment that escalates from doubt to proof to a loop. An ordinary repeating day -> a repeated passer-by (deja vu as a glitch) -> a reset seen through her own eyes -> a closer look shows a placeholder -> the render collapses -> the void behind it -> someone edits the world -> pull back to the player -> the player's own world glitches -> payoff: he is on Mia's screen. Nobody can tell who is the base reality.
Recurring devices: the neighbor (the repeated NPC who becomes a wireframe and is deleted); screens inside screens (the CRT shows the grid, Mia's monitor shows the player), each entered by a pull-back.

**Shot 1**
- Voiceover: "Every day: the same sun, the same walk, the same coffee."
- On screen: time-lapse sky whose days speed up (`dayDur` keys); Mia walks with her mug; DAY 1,197 spins to DAY 1,204.

**Shot 2** 🎬
- Voiceover: "Then the small things start. The same man walks past. Twice."
- On screen: town square; the neighbor walks past, a short glitch and RGB split, and the same neighbor walks past again; Mia side-eyes; TWICE? slams in.

**Shot 3** 🎬
- Voiceover: "She blinks. The dog is back where it started."
- On screen: Mia's point of view through her eyelids; a dog trots across the park; a blink; the dog is back at the start; label RESET?

**Shot 4**
- Voiceover: "Look closer, and the neighbor is just a placeholder."
- On screen: peephole in; a magnifier glides over the neighbor's face and shows an x-ray dummy; nameplate NON-PLAYER CHARACTER, NPC #12; Mia gasps.

**Shot 5** 🎬
- Voiceover: "Then the world simply stops rendering."
- On screen: a pink wash creeps over the square and a cross-hatch net closes over everything; Mia trembles.

**Shot 6** 🎬
- Voiceover: "Behind the scenery there is nothing. Just a grid."
- On screen: the out-of-bounds void, a grid floor rushing under Mia as she runs; two neighbors stand frozen as blue wireframes; arched title OUT OF BOUNDS.

**Shot 7** 🎬
- Voiceover: "And someone is editing it."
- On screen: a giant cursor drag-selects a wireframe neighbor and deletes him (DELETED); then it hovers over Mia, now tagged P1.

**Shot 8**
- Voiceover: "Somewhere, a player sits at a screen."
- On screen: pull back out of a CRT that shows shot 7; a player in a neon gamer room holds a remote and smirks; label THE PLAYER.

**Shot 9** 🎬
- Voiceover: "Until his own world glitches too."
- On screen: a huge silhouette of Mia rises on his wall with red eyes; a dutch tilt, RGB split and a corrupt glitch; an NPC tag lands on the player; his jaw drops.

**Shot 10** 🎬
- Voiceover: "So who is playing whom?"
- On screen: pixelate cut, then pull back out of another screen: the player's world is on Mia's monitor; Mia sips her coffee and smirks; title WHO IS PLAYING WHOM?
