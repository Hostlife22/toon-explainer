# The Great Emu War (landscape, 16:9)

Central question: how does a modern army lose a war to a flock of birds?
Arc: escalation ladder with a reversal. The emus invade (cause) -> the farmers call the army -> the army arrives with machine guns -> attack one: the gun jams -> attack two: a truck chase, the emus are faster -> a month on the map, every attack misses and the emus take the region -> weeks pass, bullets run out -> the army surrenders -> payoff: the emus get the medal.
Recurring device: a quest chip MISSION: STOP THE EMUS stays on screen from the army's arrival to the retreat and never turns green. The wheat field returns in changed states (day, time-lapse camp, dusk retreat).
Based on the 1932 episode in Western Australia, told as comedy: generic soldiers, no real people, violence only implied (misses, blasts turning to smoke) and the emus win. The emus are house-style SVG cutouts in `emu/` (the engine has no emu creature); `image` frames swap between two run poses.

**Shot 1**
- Voiceover: "Western Australia, 1932. Twenty thousand emus march into the wheat."
- On screen: a wheat field under a blue sky; emus sprint in from the right and start pecking; the counter slams 20,000 EMUS.

**Shot 2**
- Voiceover: "They eat everything. The farmers beg the army for help."
- On screen: a crimson ray burst; two newspapers slap in 0.5 s apart, EMUS EAT EVERYTHING and FARMERS CALL THE ARMY; the farmer screams.

**Shot 3**
- Voiceover: "The army sends soldiers. And two machine guns."
- On screen: a game splash: THE ARMY JOINS THE BATTLE!; a quest chip appears in the corner and stays: MISSION: STOP THE EMUS.

**Shot 4**
- Voiceover: "Day one. They open fire... and the gun jams."
- On screen: a soldier behind sandbags fires three bursts at the far-off flock; CLICK; an error popup GUN JAMMED; the emus do not even look up.

**Shot 5**
- Voiceover: "So they chase the emus in a truck. Emus run at fifty kilometres an hour."
- On screen: desert plain; the army car bounces after the flock, the emus pull away and leave it in their dust; label 50 KM/H.

**Shot 6**
- Voiceover: "For a month the army chases flocks across the map. The emus simply scatter."
- On screen: an atlas map of the wheatbelt; a brawl cloud of tin hats zigzags across it, dropping blasts that turn to smoke; X marks for every miss; then the emus' brown blotches take the whole region: EMU LAND.

**Shot 7**
- Voiceover: "Weeks pass. Ten thousand bullets. The emus barely notice."
- On screen: days flash by faster and faster over the camp; the slumped soldiers by their tent, the calendar tears, the bullet counter races; the emus hop about in the background.

**Shot 8**
- Voiceover: "In the end, the army gives up."
- On screen: dusk; the soldiers wave white flags and sweat; MISSION FAILED glitches in; they zip off, leaving dust; one emu stands where they were.

**Shot 9**
- Voiceover: "The Great Emu War. The winner: the emus."
- On screen: a gold burst; the emu stands proud with a WAR HERO rosette pinned to its chest; confetti; title THE EMUS WON.
