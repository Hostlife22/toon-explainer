# Why Volcanoes Erupt (landscape, 16:9)

Central question: why does a mountain that slept for six hundred years suddenly explode, and why do people move back?
Arc: mechanism with a disaster turn and a hopeful payoff. A calm state (the sleeping mountain) -> the hidden step made visible (magma and gas in a cross-section) -> pressure builds -> warning signs over weeks -> the release (eruption) -> human stakes (everyone runs) -> scale (the ash map) -> time skip, the same view turned green -> payoff: one split image, destroyer and gardener are the same mountain.
Recurring devices: the personified mountain whose face tracks the story (sleepy, worried, strain, angry, happy) and the same village framing reused before and after.

**Shot 1**
- Voiceover: "The village of Ashfield has slept under its mountain for six hundred years."
- On screen: a smiling-asleep mountain puffs a wisp; a farmer naps in a deckchair (zzz), a cow and a sheep graze by the ASHFIELD sign; label 600 YEARS ASLEEP.

**Shot 2**
- Voiceover: "But deep below, rock melts into magma, full of trapped gas."
- On screen: whip up to a blueprint; the cone, the pipe and the magma chamber draw themselves (MAGMA, GROUND); the board wipes and redraws gas bubbles and an arrow up (GAS); label 3 KM DOWN.

**Shot 3** 🎬
- Voiceover: "The gas pushes harder and harder, and the pressure climbs."
- On screen: close on the mountain at dusk; its face goes from worried to strain, it trembles and sweats, smoke puffs; a vertical PRESSURE meter climbs into the red and blinks.

**Shot 4**
- Voiceover: "For weeks the ground shook, and the birds left first."
- On screen: an accelerating time-lapse of days and nights; flocks fly away; three jolts shake the frame; granny shades her eyes, a kid points up; label WEEKS OF TREMORS.

**Shot 5** 🎬
- Voiceover: "Then, all at once, the mountain blew its top."
- On screen: a held beat, then on "blew" a mushroom cloud, explosion, fire and rays burst from the crater; red flash, punch and heavy shake; KA-BOOM; the farmer throws up his hands.

**Shot 6** 🎬
- Voiceover: "Ash rained down, and everyone ran."
- On screen: burning plain under a smoke ceiling, ash falling; villagers sprint right to left with screaming faces, a sheep bolts, a crowd flees behind; RUN! slams.

**Shot 7**
- Voiceover: "The ash cloud spread a hundred kilometres, burying the valley."
- On screen: zoom-punch to a map; MT. EMBER and ASHFIELD pop in, 1925 stamps; a grey ash blot spreads from the volcano over the green valley and gets a sad face; villages catch fire, red escape routes draw out; label 100 KM OF ASH.

**Shot 8**
- Voiceover: "Fifty years later, that ash had become the richest soil in the land."
- On screen: black beat with 50 YEARS LATER; the shot 1 view again, but the mountain is green and smiling, rice terraces, flowers pop up, birds return, a kid cheers and the farmer holds a pitchfork.

**Shot 9** 🎬
- Voiceover: "The mountain that buried the valley is also what feeds it."
- On screen: split screen with a jagged seam: the eruption frame (1925: IT DESTROYS) beside the green frame (1975: IT FEEDS); title WHY VOLCANOES ERUPT.
