# The Honey That Never Spoils (vertical short, 9:16)

Central question: how can food sit in a tomb for 3,000 years and still be safe to eat?
Arc: mechanism with a payoff. The jar in the tomb (hook) -> the claim: still edible -> 3,000 years pass -> where honey comes from -> bacteria need water, honey has almost none -> the sugar dries them out -> the full defence checklist -> someone tastes it -> best before: never -> twist: the original owner wants it back.
Recurring device: the torchlit corridor returns for the tasting and for the last gag; the jar is the same shape in every shot (stone in the time-lapse, personified in the science beat).
The jar is a `region` prop with a hand-drawn amphora outline; the bee is a bean in a striped outfit with insect wings (no bee creature exists).

**Shot 1** 🎬
- Voiceover: "This honey is three thousand years old."
- On screen: a torchlit tomb corridor; the archaeologist's torch pool finds a glowing jar; she gasps. Pinned: THIS HONEY IS 3,000 YEARS OLD.

**Shot 2** 🎬
- Voiceover: "And you could still eat it."
- On screen: a god beam lands on the jar, it sparkles; the archaeologist peeks in, jaw dropped. STILL EDIBLE.

**Shot 3** 🎬
- Voiceover: "It sat sealed in a tomb while three thousand years went by."
- On screen: cloud wipe to a desert tomb under a time-lapse sky; sun and moon whirl faster and faster while a counter races to 3,000 YEARS.

**Shot 4**
- Voiceover: "Bees make it from flower nectar..."
- On screen: swirl to a flower collage; a bee flutters from flower to flower. NECTAR, + BEE ENZYMES.

**Shot 5** 🎬
- Voiceover: "Bacteria need water to live. Honey is only seventeen percent water."
- On screen: a germ marches up to a personified jar; the jar's picture bubble shows germ -> water, and the water gets struck out; 17% WATER; the jar crosses its arms, smug.

**Shot 6** 🎬
- Voiceover: "Worse, the sugar sucks the water right out of them."
- On screen: honey floods up around the germ; it panics and crumbles into ash. DRIED OUT.

**Shot 7**
- Voiceover: "Low water, acidic, a dash of natural peroxide. It's armour."
- On screen: a checklist ticks LOW WATER, ACIDIC, PEROXIDE; a stamp slams UNSPOILABLE.

**Shot 8** 🎬
- Voiceover: "So of course, somebody had to taste it."
- On screen: back in the corridor; the archaeologist tastes, sweating... then star eyes and a hop. MMM!

**Shot 9** 🎬
- Voiceover: "Best before... never."
- On screen: gold sunburst; a BEST BEFORE sign shows 1200 BC, the year is struck and NEVER lands; stamp STILL GOOD.

**Shot 10**
- Voiceover: "Its original owner disagrees."
- On screen: a skeleton pops into the dark tomb, fuming and pointing: MY SNACK! Hard stop.
