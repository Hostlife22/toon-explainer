# How Coffee Conquered the World

Spine: a berry that a goat found first goes from a herder's snack to the world's habit, beating a ban, a devil scare and a monopoly.
Frame device: none (short film). Recurring devices: the running cup counter (`spec.overlays`), the goat (opening gag and payoff), the map spine.
