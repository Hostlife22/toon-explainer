# The Museum Heist (landscape, 16:9)

Central question: a three-person crew plans the perfect museum heist. Who actually walks away with the ruby?
Arc: heist with a double-cross. The plan (blueprint, erased and redrawn) -> the crew -> the break-in (security camera) -> the swap (hook takes the real ruby, a claw lowers a fake) -> the turn: the boss poofs into a guard's uniform and hits the alarm herself -> the crew is caged -> the newspaper says the ruby is safe -> payoff: the boss has the real one, the museum keeps the fake.
Recurring devices: a quest chip "STEAL THE RUBY STAR" that stays on screen and only ticks when the boss alone holds the ruby; the guard uniform (the disguise that turns her into the person who catches her own crew); the alarm strobe that runs across the cut from the button to the cage.
Showcase: blueprint sketches with `clear` and redraw, giant hand, game markers and notice log, `cctv` with `crt-cyan` and a dutch angle, hook `arm` with `take`, `claw-crane` with a load, `glow`, `poof` + look `swap`, `big-button`, `warning`, alarm overlay, `exclaim` marks, `sweat-spray`, run + `speedlines`, `cage` drop, `peek` entrance, shout `bubble`, spinning `newspaper` + `stamp`, harbour at sunset with `golden-hour`, jagged split `panel`, `tbc`; transitions `pixelate`, `whip`, `zoom-punch`, `wave`, `torn-split`.
