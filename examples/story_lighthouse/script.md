# The Last Lighthouse Keeper (landscape, 16:9)

Central question: who keeps the light burning on a rock where nobody has lived for thirty years?
Arc: myth / drama arc with a horror turn. A routine (the light, the log) -> the routine breaks (a fourth line, someone on the stairs) -> dread hold, then the jump scare -> the turn: the "monster" was the new keeper, and the room is empty -> the twist: the old keeper vanished in 1894 -> the log writes itself, the ghost is still here -> payoff: the opening image, now with two ghostly keepers.
Recurring devices: the keeper's log (a checklist that gains a line each time it returns) and the lighthouse beam (opening and closing image). Tone: slow push-ins and holds before the snap; one jump scare, deflated by the reveal within 3 s.

**Shot 1**
- Voiceover: "For a hundred years, the light on Black Rock has never missed a night."
- On screen: storm at sea, rain, a steamer tossing near a whirlpool; the lighthouse beam sweeps across the water; lightning and a flash on "night"; title THE LAST KEEPER.

**Shot 2**
- Voiceover: "Every night, old Silas writes the same three lines."
- On screen: candle-lit room; Silas raises his candle; the log KEEPER'S LOG pops in and LAMP LIT, SHIPS SAFE, ALONE tick one by one.

**Shot 3**
- Voiceover: "Tonight he adds a fourth line: someone is on the stairs."
- On screen: same room, horror grade, slow push-in; the door behind him creaks open on a cold stairwell, a silhouette fades in, red eyes blink on; Silas starts to tremble.

**Shot 4** 🎬
- Voiceover: (a crash)
- On screen: hard cut to black; a horned shadow swells, giant red eyes, a red flash, the frame shakes and splits into colour ghosts.

**Shot 5** 🎬
- Voiceover: "He shut his eyes..."
- On screen: screaming close-up in a red-only world, the camera tilts; red eyes at the window; his eyelids close over the frame.

**Shot 6** 🎬
- Voiceover: "...but it was only the new keeper. And the room was empty."
- On screen: a torch orb opens the dark; the relief keeper of 1924 holds a torch; cobwebs and a spider, an empty armchair; his face snaps to shock; labels THE NEW KEEPER, EMPTY.

**Shot 7**
- Voiceover: "Silas vanished in the great storm of 1894."
- On screen: sepia storm; a newspaper KEEPER LOST IN STORM with Silas's photo; a MISSING stamp slams with a camera punch.

**Shot 8** 🎬
- Voiceover: "Yet tonight's entry is in fresh ink: not alone anymore."
- On screen: the log returns, LOG, TONIGHT, and its last line NOT ALONE ANYMORE ticks by itself; the relief keeper goes hollow-eyed; focus racks to the smiling, waving ghost of Silas behind him.

**Shot 9** 🎬
- Voiceover: "The light on Black Rock still turns. They say it has two keepers now."
- On screen: bats sweep the frame; the calm night rock, mist, the beam sweeping again; two small ghost keepers fade in on the rock; title THE LIGHT STILL TURNS.
