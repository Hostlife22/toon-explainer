# Why Cats Knock Things Off Tables (vertical short, 9:16)

Central question: why does a cat push your cup off the table, every single time?
Arc: case loop with a twist. The stare (hook) -> tap, tap, push -> the hunter's reflex (flashback) -> the push as an experiment (diagram) -> the slow-motion fall -> the crash -> the owner comes running -> the doubt-answer: experts say attention -> twist: the human is the one being trained -> loop back to frame 1 with a new cup.
Recurring device: the parlour table and the teacup; the last shot repeats the first composition with one change (the cat now wears a crown).
The cat is a bean with orange fur, devil-horn ears and a devil tail recoloured orange: the engine has no cat creature.

**Shot 1** 🎬
- Voiceover: "Your cat is staring at your cup."
- On screen: the cat stands on the dinner table beside a teacup; a slow push-in toward the cup; the cat's eyes slide sideways on "staring". Pinned: WHY DO CATS / DO THIS?

**Shot 2** 🎬
- Voiceover: "Tap. Tap. Push."
- On screen: letterboxed extreme close-up; the paw reaches the cup twice (camera jump-ins), the face turns to menace and the frame tilts on "Push".

**Shot 3** 🎬
- Voiceover: "Blame its wild ancestors: hunters poke things to see if they run."
- On screen: swirl into a sepia forest flashback; a striped wild cat crouches in tall grass, pounces on "run", the rabbit zips away. Pinned: 10,000 YEARS AGO / WILD CAT, then POKE = TEST.

**Shot 4**
- Voiceover: "Does it move? Does it fall? Every push is an experiment."
- On screen: a blueprint board: table edge, cup, PUSH arrow and the fall arc draw in; FALL? is wiped and DATA! is chalked on "experiment".

**Shot 5** 🎬
- Voiceover: "And gravity never fails. Slowly..."
- On screen: slow motion: the cup tips off the edge and turns as it sinks; a slow LCD timer and a height counter falling from 80 CM to 0.

**Shot 6** 🎬
- Voiceover: "CRASH."
- On screen: the cup hits the floor and splits in two (OOPS on the seam) under a CRASH! cloud; flash and shake.

**Shot 7** 🎬
- Voiceover: "Then someone comes running."
- On screen: the owner peeks in: jaw drop, then rage with a hair smear and a shout bubble MY CUP!!

**Shot 8**
- Voiceover: "Experts have a theory: it's not physics. It's attention."
- On screen: a newspaper on a blue swirl, THE DAILY PURR: IT'S FOR ATTENTION, with the cat's photo.

**Shot 9** 🎬
- Voiceover: "Because every time it falls, you come running. Who trained whom?"
- On screen: the owner kneels and sweeps; a crown drops onto the cat, whose eyes turn to stars; a HUMAN TRAINED meter fills; WHO'S THE PET?

**Shot 10**
- Voiceover: "Anyway. New cup."
- On screen: callback to shot 1: a fresh cup drops onto the table, the crowned cat's eyes glow evil; AGAIN. Hard stop.
