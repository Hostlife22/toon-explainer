# The Island Nobody Wanted (landscape, 16:9)

Central question: why would two kingdoms fight for a hundred years over a rock nobody can live on?
Arc: escalation ladder with a twist. A worthless island between two kingdoms -> both claim it -> war, again and again -> a hundred years pass -> a treaty splits it in half -> twist: the island wakes up, it is a giant turtle -> payoff: it swims away and nobody gets it.
Recurring device: the same sea composition (b02, b08) and the same flat map with persistent bruises (b04, b09). Grumbria (orange and teal) and Plonkia (purple and lime) are invented kingdoms with invented flags.

**Shot 1**
- Voiceover: "Between two kingdoms lies one tiny island."
- On screen: a spinning globe; orange Grumbria and purple Plonkia face each other across the sea; a pin drops on the speck between them: THE ISLAND.

**Shot 2**
- Voiceover: "Nothing grows there. Nobody lives there."
- On screen: the locator badge grows into the island itself: a sandy lump asleep in a turquoise sea; a gull circles; POPULATION: 0.

**Shot 3**
- Voiceover: "So naturally, both kingdoms want it."
- On screen: a tilted map board: the two kings pop up on their lands; two block arrows slam into the island, MINE! and MINE!; the island's face goes worried.

**Shot 4**
- Voiceover: "They fight over it. Again. And again."
- On screen: the flat map: fleets sail from both coasts; a brawl cloud of orange and purple helmets rolls across the island and back, dropping blasts that turn to smoke; both kingdoms end up bruised.

**Shot 5**
- Voiceover: "A hundred years of arguing."
- On screen: time-lapse over the island: days flash by faster and faster; the flag on it flips orange, purple, orange; the year counter races to 100.

**Shot 6**
- Voiceover: "Finally, a treaty. Half each."
- On screen: a palace hall; the treaty slaps down between the two kings, HALF EACH; two stamps slam, SIGNED and AGREED; thumbs up.

**Shot 7**
- Voiceover: "The island is cut in two. Peace!"
- On screen: close on the island on the map: it cracks down the middle, the halves turn orange and purple and drift apart, both smiling; a flag on each half.

**Shot 8**
- Voiceover: "Then the island woke up."
- On screen: the same sea as before; the island trembles, RUMBLE, its eyes snap open; a giant turtle rises underneath it, grins and swims off with both flags on its back: PLOT TWIST.

**Shot 9**
- Voiceover: "It swam away. And nobody got the island."
- On screen: back on the flat map, both kingdoms still bruised: the two island halves drift off the edge; the kingdoms gasp; two hook arrows reach after it: COME BACK!
