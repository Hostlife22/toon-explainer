# Why Printing Money Raises Prices (landscape, 16:9)

Central question: if a king can print as many coins as he wants, why doesn't everyone get richer?
Arc: escalation ladder. A stable price -> the king prints coins (cause) -> everyone has more coins but the same bread -> the baker raises the price (effect, same frame) -> the king prints more, twice (each round bigger, the price counter doubles) -> a cart of coins for one loaf -> the comparison chart -> payoff: money is a ticket for a share of the bread.
Recurring device: a price counter that stays on screen across cuts; the palace and the bakery are reused in changed states.

**Shot 1**
- Voiceover: "In a small town, a loaf of bread costs one coin."
- On screen: the baker holds a loaf by his signpost BREAD: 1 COIN; the price counter appears.

**Shot 2**
- Voiceover: "The king needs money for a war."
- On screen: the king in his palace clutches his head; label WAR COSTS.

**Shot 3** 🎬
- Voiceover: "So he simply prints more coins."
- On screen: same palace; a heap of coins drops in (shadow first), paper money swirls, the king cheers.

**Shot 4** 🎬
- Voiceover: "Now everyone has twice as many coins."
- On screen: a cheering crowd waving coins; counter label 2x COINS.

**Shot 5**
- Voiceover: "But the town still bakes the same number of loaves."
- On screen: back at the bakery, same framing as shot 1; label SAME BREAD.

**Shot 6** 🎬
- Voiceover: "So the baker does the obvious thing."
- On screen: the baker smirks; the signpost turns to BREAD: 2 COINS and the counter jumps on the same frame.

**Shot 7** 🎬
- Voiceover: "The king prints more. The price doubles again."
- On screen: the palace again, a bigger heap; the counter jumps to 4 with a punch.

**Shot 8** 🎬
- Voiceover: "And again."
- On screen: the heap is now huge and buries the king to his chin; the counter jumps to 8.

**Shot 9** 🎬
- Voiceover: "Soon it takes a cart full of coins to buy one loaf."
- On screen: a citizen pushes a cart heaped with coins to the baker and leaves holding one tiny loaf; the counter jumps to 100.

**Shot 10**
- Voiceover: "The coins multiplied. The bread did not."
- On screen: a bar chart of coins per loaf (1, 2, 4, 8, then 100); under it five identical loaves pop in: SAME LOAF EVERY TIME.

**Shot 11**
- Voiceover: "Money isn't wealth. It's a ticket for a share of the bread."
- On screen: a gold burst; the loaf itself, crowned, flexes; a title.
